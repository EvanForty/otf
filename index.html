    Connect market makers, liquidity providers and trading agents.

Exchange factory

Anyone can launch a tokenized trading venue in minutes. A community could launch its coin with a facility that specializes in Solana memecoins.

The facility pulls in liquidity from several sources, matches compatible orders and routes trades through its execution engine. Traders use its own interface while market makers compete to supply liquidity, and part of net trading revenue buys back the community's coin. Every facility gets a public dashboard showing:

About the name

"Organized Trading Facility" means something specific in finance.

In some jurisdictions, especially under the EU's MiFID II rules, an Organized Trading Facility is a regulated type of trading venue. A permissionless crypto venue built on OTF does not automatically qualify as one.

Running a venue may require the right regulatory structure where you operate. Trading revenue and buybacks are not guaranteed.
